Business Data Processing Addendum (Draft)

Draft template — not a signed agreement or legal advice. Business customers should request a reviewed and executed DPA before providing personal data beyond normal order details.

1. Parties and scope

This draft is intended for the farm as service provider and a business customer acting as controller, only for personal data processed to fulfil orders or respond to business inquiries. The parties must identify themselves and sign a final version for it to take effect.

2. Instructions and purpose

The farm will process submitted contact, delivery, order and inquiry data only to fulfil the requested transaction, provide support, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations, and will not sell it or use it for AI model training without a separate, explicit opt-in.

3. Confidentiality and security

The farm will restrict access to authorised personnel and use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to a small online farm business. No system is risk-free; production use should include strong credentials, HTTPS, database backups, least-privilege access and a documented incident process.

4. Subprocessors

Potential service providers are listed on the Subprocessor List. The farm should notify the customer of material changes and require appropriate confidentiality and data protection commitments from providers where available.

5. Requests, incidents and deletion

The farm will reasonably assist with verified data access, correction and deletion requests. It will notify the customer of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting the covered data without undue delay after becoming aware, subject to applicable law. Data will be deleted or returned at the end of the service except where retention is legally required.

6. International transfers and liability

Hosting or routing services may process technical data outside Nepal. The parties must assess lawful transfer requirements before signing. Liability, governing law, duration, processing details and any mandatory clauses must be completed by legal counsel for the parties' actual circumstances.
