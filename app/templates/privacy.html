Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. This is a practical notice for Shree Dogada Bhumiraj Pashupanchhi Farm. It should be reviewed by a Nepal-qualified lawyer before public launch.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, optional email, delivery address, selected delivery coordinates, order items, prices, delivery fee, payment method, optional payment reference, order status and order time. When you send an inquiry, we collect the name, phone, optional email and message you submit. The owner login also processes authentication and security data.

Why we use it

We use this information to fulfil and deliver orders, calculate delivery fees, respond to inquiries, confirm manual bank transfers, prevent abuse, maintain business records and improve site security. We do not currently operate advertising analytics or AI training on customer data. Do not submit sensitive information in free-text fields.

Browser storage and third parties

The shopping cart and privacy choice are stored in your browser's local storage. They are used for site functionality, not advertising. Checkout uses OpenStreetMap map tiles and a public OSRM routing endpoint to show a map and calculate road distance. When you use checkout, your browser connects to those services and the selected coordinates are sent to the routing endpoint. Their operators may process technical connection data under their own terms. The site currently does not intentionally load analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing, retention and security

Order and inquiry information is available to authorised farm operators and to the hosting/database providers needed to run the site. We retain records only as long as needed for delivery, customer support, business records, disputes and legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage method is perfectly secure. Use HTTPS and a persistent protected database in production.

Your choices

You may export or request deletion of an order record using the Data Export / Deletion page. The form verifies an order code and phone number. Deletion removes direct identifiers from the active order record, but limited transaction details may be retained where legally necessary. You may also contact the farm at bhattakamal780@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Children and changes

This shop is intended for people able to place orders themselves or with a responsible adult. We do not knowingly seek information from children. We may update this notice when our practices change; the date above will be revised.
